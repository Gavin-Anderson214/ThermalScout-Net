# Real-image development baseline — October 29, 2026

Run: `20261029_133235_full`. Custom 88,503-parameter detector trained from random initialization for 10 epochs on 2,001 BIRDSAI training images, with 334 development validation images. Input size: 256 × 256. Training and validation are separated by video (26 train, 6 validation videos). Official TestReal was not used.

The best checkpoint was selected by validation loss at epoch 7 (0.7147707). Final epoch training loss was 0.2659311. The earlier interrupted run is separate; these files document the completed 10-epoch run.

| Metric | Measured value |
|---|---:|
| Micro precision | 3.29% |
| Micro recall | 43.11% |
| mAP@0.50 | 8.11% |
| mAP@0.50:0.95 | 2.44% |
| CPU inference + postprocessing | 7.11 FPS |

Precision and recall use confidence 0.25 and IoU 0.50. AP uses 101-point interpolation at IoU thresholds 0.50 through 0.95 in steps of 0.05. FPS excludes file loading and dataset preprocessing; it is not end-to-end drone throughput.

| Class | Precision | Recall | AP@0.50 | True positives | False positives | Ground-truth objects |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Animal | 3.25% | 44.65% | 14.07% | 426 | 12,693 | 954 |
| Human | 6.03% | 19.35% | 2.15% | 12 | 187 | 62 |

These are development-validation baseline results, not final test accuracy. The low precision indicates many false positives. Human validation support is limited. The prototype predicts at most one box per stride-4 cell and retains at most 40 detections per image. Further error analysis and model improvements are needed before drone deployment.
